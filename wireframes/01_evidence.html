How can I fix wet gumbo spots under a pivot when the tile hasn't worked?
Expert-matchedNebraska

Hold off on re-tiling or moving soil until you have more data 1. Moving soil from high spots into wet areas strips topsoil and creates new problems 1.

Gumbo spots stay wet because they are dense, clay-rich soils with low permeability 2. Start with a soil health assessment that tests organic matter, bulk density and infiltration 1.

Avoid field operations when the soil is wet or plastic, the highest risk for traffic compaction 3.
