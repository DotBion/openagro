Abstract summary · featured source

How do farming systems compare?

Baseline models estimated lower area-based greenhouse-gas emissions in the organic systems than in the conventional system.

Read with this limit: Adding composting emissions raised the manure-based estimate. Modeled footprints from one trial do not establish reductions on other farms.

002Field trial · 2021–2024

Abstract summary · featured source

What changes with tillage and cover crops?

Cover crops supported yields under conventional tillage. No-till with cover-crop residue caused severe hemp stand loss despite some improved soil indicators.

Read with this limit: A Pennsylvania hemp–barley trial with a management trade-off; applicability depends on the crop, conditions and practices.

003Research review

Abstract summary · featured source

How do nutrients and soil biology relate to crops?

The review connects manure’s nutrient and soil benefits with risks to air, water and health, and compares management options.

Read with this limit: A synthesis of existing research, rather than a new field comparison or a newly measured effect.
