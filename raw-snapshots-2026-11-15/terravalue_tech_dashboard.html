Pilot

Put your field, your wells or your supply shed into the model.

The first pilots pair a town or natural resources district with the farmers in its source-water area. Each seat in that picture is a door in.

  • A farmer or landowner

    Field reports from your management history, and a share of any dividend before the contract is signed.

  • A town, utility or district

    Nitrate kept out of your source area, priced against the plant you hope to avoid, checked against your wells.

  • A lender, buyer or investor

    Outcomes tied to contracted payments, and a ledger that gives each outcome one owner.

We use this only to contact you about access and pilots.

A farm produces more than yield.

TerraValue measures what a field produces beyond the crop, prices it at what someone already pays, and records who paid, so the money can reach the people who manage the land.

Built on USDA SSURGO soil survey data and ERA5 climate normals. Outputs are modeled estimates against a conventional twin, not measurements, and not verified credits.
