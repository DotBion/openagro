Is it for you?
✓
Open to farms in 
every state
✓
For 
producer, Farmers and ranchers age 40 or younger as of December 31, 2027, actively producing food or fiber during the 2027 season on a farm or ranch in a U.S. state or territory
✓
Pays 
$10,000 - $50,000
Awards of $10,000-$50,000 include technical-assistance costs. Every awardee must work with a qualified technical assistance provider, but no provider is required at application. Funds support one to four new conservation practices implemented in 2027; grants are disbursed in April 2027. Practices implemented on the same land during 2026 are ineligible.
Applicant must be 40 or younger on December 31, 2027. For shared farms/ranches, the majority of the business, cooperative or decision-makers should also be 40 or younger. Food or fiber production must be a primary or significant purpose. Projects include one to four conservation practices new on the project land; a practice used there during the 2026 growing season is ineligible. Awardees work with a qualified technical assistance provider, whose costs are included in the award; no provider need be selected before application. Beginning farmer status is encouraged, not required.
How to apply
Prepare documents
.
Gather your records (production history, maps, financials) before you apply.
Deadline window
.
Due Dec 1, 2026. Applications open October 28, 2026 and close December 1, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time. Informational webinar November 5 at 7 p.m. Eastern. Award decisions late February 2027, agreements by end of March, funds disbursed and project implementation begins April 2027.
