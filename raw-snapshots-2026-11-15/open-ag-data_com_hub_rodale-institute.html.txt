Model comparison
Abstract summary · featured source
How do farming systems compare?
Baseline models estimated lower area-based greenhouse-gas emissions in the organic systems than in the conventional system.
Read with this limit:
Adding composting emissions raised the manure-based estimate. Modeled footprints from one trial do not establish reductions on other farms.

002
Field trial · 2021–2024
Abstract summary · featured source
What changes with tillage and cover crops?
Cover crops supported yields under conventional tillage. No-till with cover-crop residue caused severe hemp stand loss despite some improved soil indicators.
Read with this limit:
A Pennsylvania hemp–barley trial with a management trade-off; applicability depends on the crop, conditions and practices.

How do nutrients and soil biology relate to crops?
