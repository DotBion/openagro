Step 01 · Location
Where is the field?
Type a ZIP code or a city. We'll pull your USDA hardiness zone and use it to tune species fit, planting windows, frost-kill timing, and the nitrogen plan.
ZIP code or City, ST
📍
Pick a location to unlock the rest of the field plan.
The ZIP or city sets hardiness, frost, and water context for every later recommendation.
Next: cropping system 
→
Built on USDA PHZM 2023 for winter hardiness, 15-year Open-Meteo ERA5 frost and water-balance normals, GeoNames postal data (CC-BY 4.0), SARE's Managing Cover Crops Profitably, Penn State and OSU N-credit calculators, Rodale Institute termination guidance, NRCS cover crop charts, and land-grant establishment/grazing/dryland water guidance. All recommendations are planning estimates — verify with your local extension service or NRCS before acting. Questions?
