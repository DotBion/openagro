State of Texas Agriculture Relief (STAR) Fund — Wildfire Assistance

Texas Department of Agriculture

What you can get

Up to 80% of eligible, documented recovery expenses. • Reimbursement after payment and review. Award ceiling varies with declaration and donations; current materials do not establish a universal dollar cap. June 2026 application guide

Readiness
Prep docs first
Who can apply
producer, Commercial agricultural producers, farms, ranches and agricultural businesses with documented recovery expenses directly caused by an eligible wildfire in a declared Texas county.
Coverage
TX
Deadline & timing
Due January 13, 2027. Ross Wildfire (Jack and Palo Pinto counties) and Fall 2026 Wildfires: January 13, 2027, 11:59 p.m. Central. Fall eligibility is limited to counties in the linked declaration; confirm the specific event/county with TDA. Earlier Panhandle and Getaway Pens/Dickens windows closed October 31, 2026; January does not extend them. TDA-GO registration requires advance approval.
Eligibility
Backyard gardens and hobby farms excluded. One application per operation and one award per 24 months; insurance or government-funded expenses cannot be reimbursed again. Drought is ineligible. Damages and paid expenses require documentation; leased property applications require owner authorization.
