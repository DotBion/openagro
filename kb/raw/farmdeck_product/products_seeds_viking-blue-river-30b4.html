30B4

Viking Seed · Blue River Organic Seed · Soybean · 3 MG

SeedsSoybeanConventional + organicMG 3.0Non-GMO

What the research measured

4 university trial records across IL, OH in 2024. Yield was above the trial mean in 3 of 4 records with a reported yield and trial mean. Differences may reflect site and management conditions.

4 trial records · 1 season (2024) · IL, OH. 2 named locations in the loaded data.6 independent sources.

What it is. A maturity group 3.0 conventional (non-GMO) soybean from the Albert Lea Seed family of brands, entered in 2024 in Illinois Regions 1 and 2 and in Ohio’s central early and north early trials.

Study conditions and limitations
  • Entered in conventional trials. Ask the supplier about untreated or organic seed lots.
  • Illinois Region 1 was a low finish against the trial mean; the other three records were above it. Read each trial, not the count.
  • Weed control is the constraint with non-GMO entries; budget for it.
  • One season of public data so far.
